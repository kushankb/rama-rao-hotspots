Where each Rama Rao hazard bites

Change in each of the 15 future-hazard indicators from Rama Rao et al. (2020), by district, from the INDRA-CMIP6 14-model ensemble (baseline 1985–2014). Colours are oriented so that red is always worse for agriculture and blue better. Outlined districts are hotspots: the 10% with the most adverse change, counted only where the change is actually adverse.

Districts at least 10% worse than their historical mean

For each district, the change in the indicator relative to its own 1985–2014 mean, (future − historical) / historical. A district counts when that relative change is 10% or more in the harmful direction: a fall for rainfall and rainy days, a rise for the other indicators. Districts whose historical mean is zero have no relative change and are left out of that indicator's count.

Method. Each value is the district mean of the 0.1° INDRA-CMIP6 ensemble over the future window minus the 1985–2014 baseline (percent change for rainfall and rainy days). Unusually hot and cold days use ±2 standard deviations of the baseline daily anomaly instead of the Atlas's ±4 °C, and drought proneness is proxied by SPI-3 drought frequency, because averaging 14 models removes most day-to-day and year-to-year variability. Windows: ~2050 = 2036–2065, ~2100 = 2071–2100.

Source: Rama Rao C.A. et al. (2020), Atlas on Risk and Vulnerability of Indian Agriculture to Climate Change, ICAR-CRIDA, Tables 1 and 5c and section 4.4.
